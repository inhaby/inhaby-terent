## The Trust Crisis in Indian Rentals

Renting an apartment in cities like Bangalore, Mumbai, or Pune often involves significant trust issues. Tenants are frequently asked to pay token money or deposit fees before viewing properties, making them vulnerable to scams:
- **Sublet Scams**: Fraudsters rent a property under a short lease, copy the keys, list it online, collect security deposits from multiple renters, and disappear.
- **Title Scams**: Individuals claim ownership of a family property without the legal right to lease it, leading to tenancy disputes.
- **Stock Photo Deceptions**: Listings use model home photos or CGI renders that do not match the actual property conditions.

## The Inhaby Verification Approach

To build a trusted ecosystem, Inhaby enforces a strict verification process for listing approvals. Landlords must provide proof of ownership before a property goes live.

### 1. Landlord KYC Match
We verify the uploader's identity using Aadhaar or Passport checks. We check that the landlord's name matches the owner listed on the property deeds.

### 2. Legal Title Check
Landlords must upload supporting ownership documents:
- **Registered Sale Deed**: Verifies the legal purchase of the property.
- **Municipal Property Tax Receipt**: Confirms ownership with local municipal databases (such as BBMP or BMC) and shows tax payment status.
- **Registered Power of Attorney (PoA)**: If a representative manages the property, we verify they have the legal authority to sign lease agreements and collect rent.

### 3. Utility Bill Verification
We cross-check recent utility bills (electricity, water, piped gas) to confirm the billing details match the landlord's identity.

---

## Lessons Learned

Automated identity checks (like OTP verifications) are not enough to prevent listing fraud. Verifying legal ownership documents helps reduce scams and ensures listings are genuine.
